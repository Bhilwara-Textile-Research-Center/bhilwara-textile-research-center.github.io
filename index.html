Leading Textile Testing Laboratory in Bhilwara

Bhilwara Textile Research Center (BTRC) is an ISO certified textile testing laboratory committed to delivering accurate, reliable, and high-quality testing services. Our experienced professionals use advanced technology and standardized testing methods to ensure compliance with national and international quality standards.

We assist both small-scale and large-scale textile industries in improving product quality, production efficiency, and regulatory compliance. Our state-of-the-art laboratory infrastructure supports a wide range of textile testing and consultancy services.
